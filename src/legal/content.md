## Privacy

Monospaced is the data controller for the personal data described in this notice,
and is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

### What we collect, and why

**Email enquiries.** If you email us, we hold your name, email address and the
contents of your correspondence. We use this to respond to you and, where relevant,
to manage a working relationship. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in
responding to enquiries and conducting business; where an enquiry leads to work, we
also process information as necessary to enter into and perform a contract. We review
stored correspondence at least once a year: enquiries that don't lead to work are
deleted within two years, and correspondence connected to client work is kept for
the duration of the relationship and up to six years afterwards, in line with legal
and accounting requirements. Email is hosted by Google Workspace, which processes
correspondence on our behalf.

**Server logs.** This site is hosted by [Netlify](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/),
which collects standard access logs — including visitors' IP addresses — for a
limited period, for security and operational purposes. Netlify processes this data
on our behalf under its data processing agreement. Netlify is a US company; transfers
are safeguarded under its DPA.

That's all. This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. We do not sell or share
personal data with anyone else, and we collect nothing beyond the two categories above.

### Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or request deletion of your personal data, to
object to or restrict our processing of it, and to data portability. To exercise any
of these, email [scott@monospaced.com](mailto:scott@monospaced.com). You also have
the right to complain to the [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

Monospaced is the independent UI consultancy of [Scott Boyle](https://scottboyle.uk),
a principal design engineer and senior front-end developer with 20+ years' experience
at the intersection of brand, design and technology.

First established in 2007, Monospaced spent fifteen years delivering front-end and UI
work for agencies, startups and large organisations including Thomson Reuters, Just Eat
and BT. From 2020 to 2026, Scott co-owned [Measured](https://measured.co), a digital
product consultancy, before relaunching Monospaced as an independent practice.

Monospaced takes its name from the fixed-width typefaces used in computer terminals and code editors.

This site is designed and built with [Set](https://set.monospaced.com), Monospaced's
own design system.

Monospaced is based in London, UK.
